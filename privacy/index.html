Privacy Policy

Korea Easy Go · Threes Partners · Effective 2026-10-20 (version 1.1)

English한국어

Summary

  • No sign-up, no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking. Location is used only if you tap “Near me”, and only on your phone.
  • What you save in the app (hotel address, saved places, checklist, saved flights, settings) stays on your phone.
  • Only three things reach our server: requests for updated guide data, flight look-ups, and reports you choose to send.

1. Information stored only on your device

Language and season settings, your arrival checklist, saved places (including any hotel address you type), and saved flight numbers. They are kept in the app's local storage, are never sent to us, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Location (optional)

If you tap “Near me” in Find where to buy, the app asks for your approximate location once to sort nearby stations by distance. The location is used only on your phone at that moment — it is not stored and never sent to us or anyone else. You can refuse and search by station name instead.

2. Information sent to our server

WhenWhat is sentWhyHow long
The app checks for updated guide dataNo personal information (a request for the latest data)Show current fares, counters and guidesNot stored

We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or device identifiers. Please do not include personal information in report messages. Like any internet service, our hosting provider may briefly keep technical connection logs (such as IP address and time) for security and operation.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase, Inc. — server and database hosting (data stored in the Seoul, Korea region).
  • Incheon International Airport Corporation (Korea Public Data Portal) — receives only the flight number and date for flight look-ups.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

4. Opening other apps

When you tap "Open in NAVER Map" or "Open in Google Maps", the place name is passed to that app or website. Their own privacy policies apply from then on.

5. Children

The app is a general travel guide and is not directed at children under 14. It does not knowingly collect children's information.

6. Your choices and rights

Device data: delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Reports: because reports are anonymous, tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will find and delete it. Contact us for any request about your information.

7. Security

All communication uses HTTPS. Server access is limited to authorised administrators who sign in with two-step verification.

8. Contact

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and notice important changes in the app.
