Privacy Policy

Korea Easy Go · Threes Partners · Effective 2026-10-29 (version 1.4)

English한국어

Summary

  • No sign-up, no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking. Location is used only if you tap “Near me”, and only on your phone.
  • What you save in the app (hotel address, saved places, saved tourist attractions, checklist, saved flights, settings) stays on your phone.
  • Only six things reach our server: requests for updated guide data, tourist attraction information, restaurant and café searches you start, flight look-ups, hotel address searches you start, and reports you choose to send.

1. Information stored only on your device

Language and season settings, your arrival checklist, saved places (including any hotel address you type and, if you pick it from the official address search, its official entrance location for directions), saved tourist attractions (their name and number), and saved flight numbers. They are kept in the app's local storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are not sent to us — except the text you type when you tap “Search address” (see section 2).

Location (optional)

If you tap “Near me” in Find where to buy or in Restaurants and cafés, the app asks for your approximate location once to sort nearby stations by distance, or to pick the tourist attraction nearest to you; restaurants and cafés are then searched around that attraction. The location is used only on your phone at that moment — it is not stored and never sent to us or anyone else. You can refuse and search by station name instead.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase, Inc. — server and database hosting (data stored in the Seoul, Korea region).
  • Incheon International Airport Corporation (Korea Public Data Portal) — receives only the flight number and date for flight look-ups.
  • Ministry of the Interior and Safety (road-name address service, juso.go.kr) — receives only the address text you search for and, for directions, the building codes of the address you picked.
  • Kakao Corp. (Kakao Local API) — receives only the restaurant/café search words or the attraction's location, from our server.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

4. Opening other apps

When you tap a NAVER Map button (view a place, or directions by public transport, walking or car), the place name and its location are passed to the NAVER Map app. Your starting point is left to NAVER Map — the app does not send your location. If NAVER Map is not installed, its page in the app store opens. NAVER's own privacy policy applies from then on.

5. Children

The app is a general travel guide and is not directed at children under 14. It does not knowingly collect children's information.

6. Your choices and rights

Device data: delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Reports: because reports are anonymous, tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will find and delete it. Contact us for any request about your information.

7. Security

All communication uses HTTPS. Server access is limited to authorised administrators who sign in with two-step verification.
